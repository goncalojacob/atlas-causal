## A1. The front card sometimes greets a funder with slugs and "0 steps"

**Severity: blocks the demo** (intermittent: seen on 3 of 8 loads here).

**Seen.** `first-1280.png` (15 s budget), `first-1280-b40.png` (40 s) and `first-390.png` (phone, 15 s): the intro card reads *"Start here: how-the-colonial-war-ended-the-regime  Atlas causal · 0 steps"*, *"Accounts to read: how-the-colonial-war-ended-the-regime, who-was-buying"*, and under "What most of it hangs on": *world-war-ii, world-war-i, carnation-revolution-1974, kosovo-war, eec-accession-1986, operation-odyssey-dawn*. Behind the card the map already carries "World War I", "25 April", "Charter of the United Nations": the titles are in, the card was not redrawn. `first-1280-b5.png` (5 s) and two `--dump-dom` runs at 5 s and 40 s show the same card with proper titles and "12 steps". The same family on the graph: `lens-japan-graph.png` (`?focus=actor:japan&view=graph`, 8 s) has every node named and the chip still reads **"ACTOR loading…"**.

**Why.** `src/intro.js` draws the card at creation, before any attribute shard has landed (titles and narrative steps are attributes since I3), and is redrawn only from `redrawForShards` in `src/main.js` (about line 389), which runs once per animation frame after a landing. Whatever happens to that one frame (a redraw before `intro.refresh()` in the same function throwing, the tab being hidden when the frame is due, a landing that arrives while a redraw is already booked) leaves the card on the ids it was born with. The lens chip has the same shape. I could not run a real browser with a real clock here; the previous reviewers' `docs/screens/m86-*.png` show titles, so this is a race and not the steady state, but a funder who opens the site and gets the slug version gets it for as long as the card is open.

**Fix (lane A).** Two things. Nothing on the page prints an id where a title goes: while the title is missing print the "loading…" the chips already use (`LOADING_LABEL` in `src/attributes.js`), never `e.id`. And the intro, the chips and the composer redraw off the atlas's own attribute count (the `arrived` counter the render keys read) rather than off the landing callback: subscribe once, compare the count to the one last drawn, redraw when it moved, and check again on `visibilitychange`. A browser test at rest after `settledShards`: no `.intro-go` and no `.lens-name` text equals its `data-id` or the loading label.
