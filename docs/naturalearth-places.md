A match is never guessed. Two signals are accepted: the record's own
`wikidata` against Natural Earth's `WIKIDATAID`, and an exact fold of one of
the record's `names` against `NAME` or `NAME_EN` **where exactly one city
survives it and it is within 1° of the point the record already gives**.
A place record with no Natural Earth city is not an error: it gets no city
feature, and the label layer draws it from the place record itself.

**To resolve one of the candidates below**, add its line to the `entries` of
`data/imports/naturalearth-places.json` — keyed by the `ne_id`, with a `note`
saying why you are sure — and re-run `--places` to refresh this document and
`node tools/import/naturalearth.mjs --source vendor/natural-earth/10m` to put
the city into the base map. Nothing here is urgent: an unresolved record
costs a link between a dot and a record, and never a wrong label.

**A place inside a city is not that city.** `belem` is a parish of Lisbon,
`alvor` of Portimão and `parque-das-nacoes` of Lisbon again, and the nearest
city to each of them is the one it is *part of*. One place is one city: the
validator refuses a second entry on a city that already has one, and a line
marked ALREADY below is that trap. Such a record wants no entry at all — the
label layer draws it from its own point, which is where it actually is.
