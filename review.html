Atlas causal

The review queue: every record drafted by the assistant, until a person has read it and signed it.

synthetic fixtures — not history

This page is a maintainer's tool, not part of the atlas. It is not linked from the map, and it exists to retire one thing: the dated exception in CLAUDE.md that let an assistant draft records so the interface could be built against real material. Every record nobody has read yet says so — review.status is draft until somebody signs it — and the queue on the left is exactly that list, whoever wrote the record. Reading one, correcting what is wrong and signing it puts your name on it and the day you read it in review.signedBy.

Saving needs the local server. Run node tools/serve.mjs and open this page from http://localhost:8000/review.html: a save is validated against the whole atlas before anything touches disk, the record file is rewritten and data/index/ is rebuilt. Opened any other way — under python3 -m http.server, or on the published site, neither of which can write — every save becomes a correction bundle on your clipboard and opens the correction issue instead. Nothing is lost either way, and the public site never gains a backend.

The queue is one list, in four orders. flags puts what the validator and the drafter asked to have looked at first, degree what most of the atlas hangs on, oldest what has been waiting longest, kind the order the contribution form offers. The chips under it narrow by kind, by flag and by which writer made the record. Only the rows on screen exist: the list is as long as the queue and the page holds a screenful of it.

Claim a record if somebody else might be reading it too. It puts your name and the day on review.claimedBy, it shows in the list, and it expires after a week. It is not a lock — nothing refuses a save because of it — and signing takes it off. Beside the record: its history, out of the repository's own commits, and, once you change anything, what you have changed since the draft.

Keys: j and k (or the arrows) walk the queue, ctrl+s saves, ctrl+enter signs.
