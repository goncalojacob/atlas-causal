# Plan — 5 September 2026: the backlog after tonight's chain

Agreed with the owner in the small hours of 5 September, after M20–M29
were scheduled. Publishing, "World scope" and "Research" are out of this
plan; "Spheres of influence for 1415–1580" is deleted from the backlog.
Order chosen by the assistant (owner: "whatever makes most sense").

## Decisions taken

1. **Offices are records, not code.** A new kind `office`: owned by an
   actor (`of`), a title, `when`, and a `category` from a small closed set
   — `head-of-state`, `head-of-government`, `legislature`, `party-leadership`,
   `military-command`, `religious`, `other` — so a sultanate, a doge, a
   party's general secretary or a chief are offices of their own actor,
   added by writing a record. Portugal starts with three: monarch,
   president, prime minister.
2. **A tenure is the existing relation `led`, from a person to the
   state**, never to the regime polity of the moment (Salazar led
   `portugal`; the Estado Novo's own relation to the state carries the
   regime). `led` gains an optional `office` (an office id) and an
   optional `startedBy` (an event id: the election, coup or succession
   that began it). One strip per office on the state's card, holders as
   bars over time; an Offices grouping on the timeline, tenure bars with
   the holder's events as ticks.
3. **Rule amendment on the one-edge rule:** an event named as
   `startedBy` on a tenure counts as connected, edge or not. The
   retracted elections that produced a holder come back through it;
   elections that changed nobody stay retracted.
4. **Events inside events:** a `parent` field on the event, one parent,
   a tree; the parent carries its own dates, checked against the
   children's; no cycles. The card says "Part of"; a parent lists its
   parts in order; the timeline draws a parent as a bracket over its
   children; the graph collapses children into their parent when zoomed
   out (on top of M25's geometric stacks); an edge to a parent is an edge
   to the whole; horizon and consequences treat the inside as inside.
5. **`place` becomes optional on every event.** An unplaced event is on
   the timeline and in the graph and never on the map; the map's corner
   counts the unplaced events in the window. A polygon place ("Portugal"
   as an area) is a later, second kind of place, not now.
6. **Place names are dated.** `names` on a place gains optional `from`/`to`
   per name, so the base map labels a city by the year shown (Lourenço
   Marques until 1976, Maputo after) once somebody has written it.
7. **Roles are a closed list, in data.** `data/roles.json` holds the
   list; the validator enforces it; adding a role is an edit to that
   file, reported by the validator, never a code change. Each actor line
   on an event becomes `{ actor, role, note }`, the note free text for
   the phrase the drafting runs wrote. The mapping from the 163 roles in
   use to the list is written first, as a table the owner approves, and
   applied by a run afterwards. Starting list (27):
   leader, head-of-state, head-of-government, minister, commander,
   government, opposition, party, candidate, elected, deposed, resigned,
   belligerent, invader, occupier, victim, perpetrator, target,
   signatory, negotiator, founder, member, applicant, host,
   author, movement, institution.
8. **Continents stop being the default lane.** The timeline opens as one
   lane, best packing; the grouping control offers actor, place, office;
   region stays a derived fact and a filter. The Russia/Turkey/Egypt
   question disappears with the default.
9. **Base map** (owner's next big project), all Natural Earth 10 m,
   fetched from the `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector` repository on GitHub
   (the only reachable host), sharded by zoom level and region, first
   view under 1 MB, **15 MB ceiling on disk** to revisit after testing:
   coastlines and land at 10 m, rivers, lakes, physical regions (mountain
   ranges, deserts, plains, peninsulas) as polygons with labels at full
   detail, peaks, and cities with population above 100 000 **plus every
   city the atlas holds a place record for**. Labels: modern name and
   local-language name from Natural Earth, dated names from the place
   record when one exists. Never roads, railways or modern regions.
10. Three code-only items stay as they are in the backlog: one renderer
    for the contribution form and the review editor; a record's history
    on the dashboard; a presence's capital as a reference to a place.

## Milestones, in order

- **M30 — the schema milestone.** `office` kind and schema; `led` with
  `office` and `startedBy`; `parent` on events with rules; `place`
  optional with the unplaced count; dated place names; `data/roles.json`
  with the list and the validator rule *reporting* (not yet failing on)
  unknown roles; the actor line's `note`; index and topology; the
  cards (tenure strips, Part of, parts list); the timeline's brackets;
  the graph's parent collapse; the contribution form and the review
  editor for all of it; `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Code
  only. The three Portuguese office records are the one exception, they
  carry no claim beyond title and category.
- **M31 — tenures and the elections, content.** Under the dated
  exception: every monarch, president and prime minister of Portugal
  from 1890 to 2025 as `led` relations with `office` and, where the
  dataset has the event, `startedBy`; the retracted elections that
  started a tenure reinstated by decision 3; new person actors only
  where a holder is missing. Marked as assistant drafts, queued for
  review.
- **M32a — the roles table**, written by the assistant locally and
  approved by the owner: 163 → 27 with the note text for each.
  **M32b — the roles applied** by a run: every event rewritten by the
  table, the validator switched to *failing* on an unknown role.
- **M33 — the lanes.** One lane by default; actor, place, office
  groupings; region as a filter; the continent code path removed.
- **M34 — the three code-only items.**
- **M35 — base map data.** The import tool: fetch, select, simplify,
  shard, budget report; the manifest; licences (Natural Earth is public
  domain). Output committed.
- **M36 — base map rendering.** Layers with zoom thresholds under the
  territories; the toggle; performance on the whole world at every zoom.
- **M37 — base map labels.** Modern, local and dated names; collision
  avoidance; the place records' cities; screenshots.

Each milestone gets a brief; M30, M35 and M36 get an Opus review of the
brief before they run. The chain runs as tonight's does: gated, claimed,
shepherded, on `m0`.
