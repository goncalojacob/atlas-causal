Bibliography

Nothing enters this atlas without a source, and a source is written once and cited by reference: fifty records resting on the same book cite one file. This list is generated from the records themselves every time the page is opened, so a count here is the count of what actually cites the work — not a number anybody maintains by hand.

A title opens the source inside the atlas, where every event, link, actor, place and territory that cites it is listed with the page or section it names. A citation made by an edge in dissent — a work arguing against a link rather than for it — is shown apart and marked there, because a book that disagrees with a claim is not evidence for it.
